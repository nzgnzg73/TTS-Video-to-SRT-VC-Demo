## 🛑 GPU & Driver Requirements

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This software utilizes heavy AI models that run on **NVIDIA Graphic Cards**.
>
> **Error Fix:** If you see `"GPU Crash: CUDA failed"`, your NVIDIA drivers are likely outdated.
>
> **Update Here:** [Latest NVIDIA Drivers](https://www.nvidia.com/download/driverResults.aspx/234720/en-us/)
>
> **System Info:** Developed on an **NVIDIA Quadro P2000** (Driver **581.80**). Works best on RTX or Quadro series.

### Step 4: 🎤 Voice-to-Voice Converter Pro

> Upload a **"target" voice** and your own **"source" voice**. The software changes your voice to match the target's tone and identity while keeping your words and delivery.
>
> **Speed:** Runs well on CPU; **lightning-fast on GPU**.

---

### Step 5: Audio/Video to SRT Transcriber

Convert speech to text with multiple model options:

| Model | Speed | Languages | GPU Required |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Small (Balanced) ⭐** | Ultra Fast | General | No |
| **Medium (Better Quality)** | Fast | 99 Languages | Recommended |
| **Large V3 (Best Quality)** | Standard | 99 Languages | **Yes** |

<details>
<summary><b>📦 Additional Models Available for Download</b></summary>
<br>

- `Large V1` — First generation large model
- `Large V2` — Second generation large model
- `Large V3 Turbo` — Speed-optimized large model
- `Distil Large V2` — Compressed for efficiency
- `Distil Medium.en` — English-specialized medium model

</details>

> **Bonus Feature:** Upload an existing `.srt` file and extract all text at once!

---

### Step 6: 🎵 Audio Cleaner & Enhancer

> Removes background noise (fans, hums, traffic) to deliver **studio-quality**, clean audio.
